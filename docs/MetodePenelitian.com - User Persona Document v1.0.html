MetodePenelitian.com - User Persona Document v1.0

Purpose: Define the primary user personas that guide product, UX, content, and feature decisions.

Persona Summary

Persona 1: Undergraduate Student

  • Goals: Complete assignments and thesis efficiently.
  • Pain Points: Difficulty finding credible references, methodology confusion, citation errors.
  • Needs: Step-by-step guides, AI assistance, templates, calculators.
  • Key Features: Knowledge Base, AI Research Assistant, Citation Generator.

Persona 2: Master's Student

  • Goals: Produce high-quality thesis and publish journals.
  • Pain Points: Research gap identification, literature review, statistical analysis.
  • Needs: Advanced AI tools, journal database, SmartPLS/SPSS guidance.
  • Key Features: AI Research Gap, Literature Mapping, Statistics Hub.

Persona 3: Doctoral Researcher

  • Goals: Conduct original research and publish in indexed journals.
  • Pain Points: Novelty, systematic reviews, international publication.
  • Needs: Advanced research workflows and collaboration.
  • Key Features: Research Repository, AI Writing Assistant, Collaboration Tools.

Persona 4: Lecturer

  • Goals: Teach research methods and supervise students.
  • Pain Points: Repetitive guidance, resource preparation.
  • Needs: Course materials, templates, assessment tools.
  • Key Features: Academy, Download Center, AI Assistant.

Persona 5: Professional Researcher

  • Goals: Manage research projects and publications.
  • Pain Points: Data organization, reference management.
  • Needs: Integrated workspace and analytics.
  • Key Features: Repository, Dashboard, AI Workspace.

Cross-Persona Design Principles

  • Minimize learning curve.
  • Prioritize trustworthy academic content.
  • Support multilingual access.
  • Provide AI assistance with human verification.
  • Optimize for desktop and mobile.
